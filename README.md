# LangLyr
A Japanese lyrics vocabulary learning app
<br>If you're like me and listen to J-pop but also learn Japanese, one of the worse things is only knowing like 10% of the words and not understanding the full thing at all. Using this app, you can learn Japanese vocab while listening to your favourite songs!
## Collections
Not only can you listen to any YouTube song or playlist, you can also choose from our curated list of artists and playlists
## Translate instantly
We use translations from jisho.org, a community database of Japanese words and meanings, so you can be assured that they are not Google Translate hallucinations
## Store for later
We keep the words you translated in a table for easy learning and reuse
